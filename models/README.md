# Printable models

Everything you print for Bolle lives here.

| folder | what |
|---|---|
| [case/v1/](case/v1/) | the case: front shell, rear shell, board holder, button and screw spacers, plus the parts list |
| [mounts/](mounts/) | mounts that hold Bolle in the car, sorted by car brand |

**What to print first:** the case. Its print plates and STLs are in [case/v1/](case/v1/), which also explains how to print them and lists every part you need to buy. The same files are attached to each [release](https://github.com/jolynstudios/openbolle/releases). Then pick a mount for your car. Each mount comes with a small click test: print that before the mount itself.
