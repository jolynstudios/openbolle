# Printable models

Everything you print for Bolle lives here.

| folder | what |
|---|---|
| [case/v1/](case/v1/) | the case: front shell, rear shell, board holder, button and screw spacers |
| [mounts/](mounts/) | mounts that hold Bolle in the car, sorted by car brand |

**What to print first:** the case. Its print plates are attached to each [release](https://github.com/jolynstudios/openbolle/releases), and [case/v1/](case/v1/) explains how to print them. Then pick a mount for your car. Each mount comes with a small click test: print that before the mount itself.
